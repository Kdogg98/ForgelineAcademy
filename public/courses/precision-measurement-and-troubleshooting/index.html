On the job

Precision Measurement & Troubleshooting is the kind of job where production wants the guard back on before lunch. That pressure is how plants skip the baseline. Take the as-found: temperature, grease condition, alignment readings, seal weep, belt tension. Write it. Then decide if the repair is a component swap or a geometry problem. Geometry problems come back on the next crew.

The useful version of Precision Measurement & Troubleshooting is the version you can run on nights without a specialist on the radio. Use dial indicators, calipers, micrometers, and laser measurement tools to diagnose mechanical problems to thousandths of an inch. Includes runout, concentricity, and soft-foot checks. Keep the as-found. Change one variable. Hand the next crew a log, not a story.
