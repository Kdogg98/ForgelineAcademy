Answers to the questions plant managers and technicians ask most about ForgeLine Academy courses, pricing, company seats and on-site training.

What does ForgeLine Academy teach?

Industrial maintenance training for millwrights, industrial electricians, I&E technicians and controls engineers. The catalog has 78 courses across four paths: Mechanical, Electrical, I&E and Engineering.

Which courses are free?

44 courses are free with an account: the full Mechanical path and the full Electrical path. Browse them in the course catalog.

What does Premium cost and what does it add?

Premium is $19.99 per month. It unlocks the I&E and Engineering paths, including instrumentation, control valves, PLC programming, DCS, networking and functional safety courses.

How do company seats work?

Companies can buy online Premium seats for their crew: Plant 5-seat is $129 per month and Plant 10-seat is $229 per month. Seats are online only and do not include a plant visit. See pricing.

Is there a free trial for companies?

Yes. A company can trial 5 online seats free for 30 days. After 30 days, choose Plant 5-seat or Plant 10-seat, or access ends. Start from the request page.

Do you offer on-site training?

Yes. I work with a network of millwrights, electricians and engineers who deliver hands-on training at your facility, built around your equipment. ForgeLine is based in Oklahoma and travels nationwide. See on-site training.

Can you help troubleshoot a problem at our plant?

Yes, plant troubleshooting support is available for recurring equipment problems such as repeat VFD trips, motor failures and control issues. See services or call +1 580 421 5714.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. You earn a certificate when you complete a course, and you can view your certificates from your account.

How are the courses delivered?

Online, in modules and lessons with quizzes. Each course page lists its modules and lessons with estimated minutes, for example Bearings, Lubrication & Alignment Fundamentals.

Does training replace our site safety program?

No. ForgeLine training supports, but does not replace, your site's lockout/tagout, electrical safety program, qualification and change-management procedures.

How do I contact ForgeLine Academy?

Email support@forgelineacademy.com or call +1 580 421 5714. You can also book a meeting.
