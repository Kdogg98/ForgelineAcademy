On the job

Engineering work on the plant floor still has to survive a night shift. Advanced PLC Programming Patterns & Standards is useful only if a technician can maintain what you design: tag names, network segments, safety functions, and a commissioning checklist that matches the as-built. Pretty drawings that do not match the panel are how plants inherit undocumented logic.
